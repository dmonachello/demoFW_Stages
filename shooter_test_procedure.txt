Shooter System Test Procedure (Leader/Follower)

Before You Start
- Put the robot on blocks or remove wheels if the shooter could interact with the field.
- No balls loaded for the first half of the tests.
- Confirm CAN IDs match Constants.Shooter.kLeaderId and kFollowerId.
- Confirm breaker sizes and wiring are correct.

1) Power-On & CAN Sanity
- Power the robot.
- Open Driver Station + SmartDashboard.
- Confirm RPM values change when you spin the flywheel by hand.
Pass criteria: Leader/follower RPM values update.

2) Direction Check (Very Low RPM)
- Press A (low RPM).
- Observe motor direction.
- Stop with X if needed.
Pass criteria: Flywheel spins in expected direction; both motors spin the same direction.
If motors fight: Toggle Constants.Shooter.kFollowerInverted.

3) Follower Verification
- Press A (low RPM).
- Compare leader RPM vs follower RPM on dashboard.
Pass criteria: Follower RPM closely tracks leader RPM.

4) PID / Setpoint Tracking (No Ball)
- Press A (low), Y (mid), B (high).
- Observe time-to-speed, overshoot, and stability.
Pass criteria: Reaches target quickly and settles within allowed error without oscillation.

5) At-Speed Indicator
- Use A/Y/B and watch Shooter At Target RPM.
Pass criteria: At Target becomes true when steady.

6) Manual Shoot Placeholder
- With shooter at speed, press Right Bumper.
- Check Shooter Ready Shot Count increments.
Pass criteria: Count increments only when At Target is true.

7) Auto-Shoot Placeholder
- Press B (high).
- Immediately press Left Bumper (auto-shoot).
- Watch Shooter Auto Shoot Requested; it should clear once ready.
- Confirm Shooter Ready Shot Count increments automatically.
Pass criteria: Auto-shoot fires as soon as speed is reached.

8) Wait-Time Metrics
- Press Left Bumper before the shooter is ready.
- Wait for it to reach speed.
- Check Shooter Wait Completed Count, Last/Total/Average Wait Time Sec.
Pass criteria: Timing values update as expected.

9) D-Pad Up (Fake Vision RPM)
- Hold D-pad up.
- Shooter should go to RPM computed from fake distance.
- Release D-pad up; setpoint stops updating (last value remains until changed).
Pass criteria: RPM setpoint changes while holding D-pad up.

10) Optional: With Ball
- Load 1 ball only.
- Spin to target RPM.
- Press Right Bumper to simulate shoot.
- Observe RPM dip and recovery.
Pass criteria: RPM recovers quickly and consistently.

What to Record
- Time to reach setpoint (low/mid/high)
- Overshoot and oscillation
- At-speed stability
- Follower mismatch, if any
